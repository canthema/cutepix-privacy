Privacy Policy – Cutepix

Last updated: November 9, 2026

This privacy policy applies to the Android app Cutepix (package com.lumi.pikselev; shown on the phone as "Cutepix", formerly "Piksel Ev"), published on Google Play by caselii design.

Summary

The app does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data. It has no Internet permission, no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting, no third-party SDKs and no user accounts. Nothing leaves your phone through this app.

Data the app accesses on your device

  • Installed apps: to work as a home screen, the app reads the names, icons and app shortcuts of the launchable apps installed on your phone (including work profile apps when visible to the launcher). This is used only to show and open them and never leaves the device. The app does not use the QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES permission; on Android 11+ it only sees launchable apps and theme/icon-pack apps through Android's package visibility queries.
  • Approximate location (optional): off by default. Only if you turn on "Use location" and confirm the in-app explanation does Android ask for approximate location (ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION). It is read only while the app is open, at most every few hours (about every 6 hours), rounded to about 10 km, stored only in the app's private settings on your phone and used only to calculate sunrise and sunset times offline. Precise location and background location are never requested. If location is off, denied, unavailable or not yet found, the city you pick in the app is used as a fallback. You can turn location off in the app or revoke the permission in Android settings at any time; turning it off deletes the stored rounded location.
  • Battery level and next alarm: read from public Android system information only to display them on the night clock; not stored.
  • Your settings and content: layout, folders, hidden apps, guest mode list, quick note, language, theme, icon choices and launch counts (for "most used" sorting) are stored only on your phone and are deleted when you clear the app's storage or uninstall the app.
  • Icon packs: if you choose an installed ADW/Nova-compatible icon pack, only its icon images and icon list are read; its code is never run and nothing is sent to it.
  • Wallpaper: with your choice, the app can set a background as the home/lock screen wallpaper using the normal SET_WALLPAPER permission (no prompt).

Permissions

  • SET_WALLPAPER – to apply the background you choose.
  • ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION – optional, see above.

No INTERNET, precise location, background location, contacts, SMS, phone, storage/media, notification listener, accessibility, device-admin or QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES permission. The optional Android screensaver (Daydream) service is only bindable by the system.

Sharing and transfers

Nothing is sent to caselii design or to any third party. If you use "Search web" or "Search Play", the text you typed is handed to your phone's browser or the Play Store app, whose own privacy policies apply. If you create a backup, the app writes your layout, folders, settings and quick note (without the rounded location) to a file you pick with Android's file picker; the app never sends that file anywhere and does not use a storage permission.

Android backup and device transfer

If Android's own backup (for example Google backup) or device-to-device transfer is turned on, Android may include the app's layout and settings file (including app open counts). Since version 3.9 the quick note and the rounded location are kept in a separate file that is excluded from Android cloud backup and device transfer. The developer never receives any of this data.

In-app copy

The same policy can be read offline inside the app (version 3.9 and later): Settings > PERMISSIONS & PRIVACY > PRIVACY POLICY.

Security, retention and deletion

All data stays in the app's private storage on your phone, protected by Android's app sandbox. You can delete it at any time by clearing the app's storage (Android Settings > Apps > Cutepix > Storage) or uninstalling the app. Backup files you created are kept wherever you saved them until you delete them.

Children

Cutepix is not designed for children. The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes
